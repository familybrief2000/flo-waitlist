The family newspaper

FLO

Stop hunting the school email.

Buried in school email. Picture day. Soccer’s at the wrong field. Two pickups at once. Flo reads the mail you already have and prints one paper for the table — so you get the morning back with the kids, not the inbox. Named after my mom. She doesn’t invent.

First families, by hand. We’ll write when a real household is using the paper — not when we have a prettier page. Your data is safe with us. Not shared with anyone else.
