The family newspaper

FLO

Stop hunting the school email.

Picture day. Wrong field. Two pickups. Already in your inbox. Already missed.

They wait for you to type.

School already sent it. Then you become the secretary: copy picture day into the family calendar, the field change into a note, both pickups by hand. Miss one forward and the board is wrong. So you still hunt the email.

You don’t type a thing.

Connect Gmail once. School, sports, and work mail come in on their own. One paper for the table. She doesn’t invent.

First families, by hand. We’ll write when a real household is using the paper.

Your data is safe with us. Not shared with anyone else.
